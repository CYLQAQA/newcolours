Short-SKU matching rule

For an eight-part stock SKU, keep parts 1, 2, 3, 5 and 7. Remove keyboard, RAM/colour and grade. Match the result against the workbook’s SKU column, together with Location. The unnamed helper column is ignored.

CP-AP-MS0022-INT-01S-3L-M1M24-W → CP-AP-MS0022-01S-M1M24

LP-AP-P17113-ENG-51S-8G-M1000-Z → LP-AP-P17113-51S-M1000

All RAM/colour and keyboard variants can match. The final grade must agree with the Grade column and be C, Z or W. Unrecognized structures, missing prices and conflicting price assignments appear in Review and are excluded from invoices.

Confirmed region and location mapping

US, UK and IE are excluded. Other unknown locations appear in Review. Future JP and DUB price rows are supported automatically.

CSV columns: SKU, location_code, Qty, Offer. Duplicate SKU + location rows are merged. Prices use “allocated price” as supplied, rounded to two decimals; no currency conversion or shipping adjustment. Imports affect this session. Reopen to return to the attached starting files.
